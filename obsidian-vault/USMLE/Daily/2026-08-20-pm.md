## 🔥 Rapid Fire Recap (High Yield Summary)
1. **Epigastric pain radiating to back + Lipase > 3x** → **Acute Pancreatitis** → **Next Step:** Fluids (LR), Pain control, NPO. (Do NOT order CT first).
2. **Acute Pancreatitis + ALT > 150** → **Gallstone Pancreatitis** → **Next Step:** RUQ Ultrasound.
3. **Pancreatitis patient + Fever/Hypotension after 10 days + CT shows gas** → **Infected Necrosis** → **Next Step:** Antibiotics (Carbapenem) + Step-up drainage.
4. **4 weeks after Pancreatitis + Early Satiety + Abdominal Mass** → **Pancreatic Pseudocyst** → **Next Step:** Symptomatic? Drainage (Cystogastrostomy). Asymptomatic? Observation.
5. **Chronic Pancreatitis + Isolated Gastric Varices** → **Splenic Vein Thrombosis** → **Tx:** Splenectomy.
6. **Sore throat (Mono) + Sudden LUQ pain/Shoulder pain** → **Splenic Rupture** → **Next Step:** Stable? CT. Unstable? OR.

## **🔥 Rapid Fire Recap (Summary)**
1. **Vignette:** CKD on EPO, decreasing Hb.
    - **Diagnosis:** Functional Iron Deficiency.
    - **Action:** Give **IV Iron** (Not oral!).
2. **Vignette:** Microcytic anemia, High Ferritin, Low TIBC.
    - **Diagnosis:** Anemia of Chronic Disease (ACD).
    - **Mechanism:** Hepcidin locking iron in macrophages.
3. **Vignette:** Microcytic anemia, RBC count is actually high or normal. Mentzer Index < 13, Target cells.
    - **Diagnosis:** **Thalassemia**.
    - **Next Step:** Hb Electrophoresis.
4. **Vignette:** Gastrectomy patient with neuropathy (tingling) & anemia.
    - **Diagnosis:** B12 Deficiency.
    - **Lab Clue:** High **Methylmalonic Acid (MMA)**.
5. **Vignette:** Treating severe B12 deficiency, patient develops palpitations/weakness.
    - **Complication:** **Hypokalemia**. (Cell turnover uses up K+).
